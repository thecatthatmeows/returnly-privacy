Returnly Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Returnly ("Returnly", "we", "us", or "our") is a warranty tracking application that helps users store product warranty information and receipts and receive reminders about their warranties.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Returnly, we may collect information that you provide to the app, including:

  • Account information, such as your email address.
  • Product and warranty information that you enter.
  • Receipt information or images that you choose to store.
  • Information required to provide notifications and reminders.

We do not collect information that you do not provide or that is not necessary for the operation of Returnly, except where described in this Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Provide and operate Returnly.
  • Store your warranty and receipt information.
  • Send warranty and reminder notifications.
  • Maintain and improve the application.
  • Protect the security and reliability of the service.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

Returnly may use third-party services to provide parts of its functionality. These services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

These services may include:

  • Firebase, for application services and notifications.
  • Supabase, for authentication and data storage.
  • RevenueCat, for subscription and in-app purchase management.
  • Google Play services, for app distribution and purchases.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored through Returnly. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate Returnly, provide notifications, process subscriptions, store data, or maintain the service.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Returnly and its features, unless you request deletion or a longer retention period is required by law.
